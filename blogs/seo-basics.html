SEO Basics for Small Businesses

Get found online with simple strategies.

1. Target the Right Keywords

Research keywords your customers use to find businesses like yours. Use tools like Google Keyword Planner to keep it simple and effective.

2. Optimize Your Website Content

Include keywords naturally in titles, headings, and text. Make sure your content is clear, helpful, and answers customer questions.

3. Claim Your Google Business Profile

Set up and verify your Google Business Profile to appear in local searches and on Google Maps—perfect for small businesses.

4. Build Quality Backlinks

Get reputable sites to link to yours through partnerships, guest posts, or local directories. It boosts your credibility with search engines.

5. Focus on Mobile Optimization

Ensure your site loads fast and looks great on phones—Google prioritizes mobile-friendly sites in its rankings.
